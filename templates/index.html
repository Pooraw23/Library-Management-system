System Capabilities & Features

📚 Direct Borrow & Return

Students can borrow available books directly, inspect due dates, and return books anytime with instant inventory updates.

⚡ Real-Time Circulation

Track book copies, place queue reservations when copies are out of stock, and calculate overdue fines at ₹2.00/day.

🛡️ Role-Based Portals

Dedicated dashboards for student members to track active loans and administrators to manage catalog collections.
